# Vocabulary
GTM (Go-to-Market): The strategic plan an organization uses to bring a product or service to market and sell it to customers.
ICP (Ideal Customer Profile): A categorical description of the perfect high-value company or organization that gets the most value from your product.
Buyer Persona: A semi-fictional representation of your ideal individual buyer based on data, demographics, behavior patterns, motivations, and goals.
Lead: An unqualified contact who has shown initial interest in your company by providing their contact information.
Prospect: A qualified lead who has been vetted, fits your target criteria, and has the budget, authority, or need to buy.Customer: An individual or organization that has officially purchased your product or service.
Revenue: The total amount of money a company actually receives from its sales of goods or services during a specific period.
Pipeline: A visual representation of where various sales opportunities sit within the stages of the sales process.
